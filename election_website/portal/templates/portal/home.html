How your vote is protected
1

You sign it

Your ballot is signed with your own Ed25519 key. Nobody can change it, or vote in your name.

2

Three parties receive it

It is sent to all three nodes at once, so no single party can hide it.

3

It is chained

Each block holds the fingerprint of the one before it. Editing an old vote breaks every link after it.

4

The majority decides

The official result comes from the copies that agree, two out of three.
